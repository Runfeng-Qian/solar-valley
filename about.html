ABOUT US & PROJECT SUMMARY

"Solar Valley Green Hydrogen Group Co., Ltd" (SVGHG)was established in April 2023 in Canada. As a professional supplier of green new energy technologies and products, we are committed to promoting the application of new renewable energy technologies worldwide and provide solutions for new renewable energy supply to countries and regions with scarce natural resources.

Our main technologies include hydrogen direct combustion power generation, solid-state hydrogen storage, molten salt concentrated solar power plants (abbreviated as CSP PLANT), and photovoltaic power plants (abbreviated as PV PLANT).

The solid-state hydrogen storage technology involves our advanced magnesium (Mg)-based solid-state hydrogen storage system. The process collects green hydrogen for solidified storage, The process is a safe, efficient, and low-cost solutions for the storage and transportation of hydrogen.

Through the hydrogen direct combustion power generation and solid-state hydrogen storage technologies, we have established a production chain of wind and solar clean electricity, green hydrogen, and hydrogen clean electricity. We will construct electric a hydrogen power plants in countries lacking traditional petrochemical resources, achieving the transformation and development towards a "zero-carbon economy."

SVGHG is also actively development Ing new applications for liquid ammonia and methanol hydrogen storage to improve hydrogen storage efficiency and further perfect the use of liquid ammonia and methanol as clean new fuels for the maritime industry.

SVGHG will explore new energy technologies designed to enhance and protect both the natural and human environment e.g. green house facilities and aqua farm operations.
